## 👋 About

I am **Supriyo Sarkar**, an M.Tech graduate in Computer Science and Engineering from **Kalyani Government Engineering College**. I enjoy working across research, machine learning and software engineering—especially where a technical idea can be tested, explained and turned into a useful implementation.

My academic and project work includes privacy-aware federated learning for medical imaging, deep learning, homomorphic encryption, quantum computing, full-stack web development and embedded systems.

- 🎓 **M.Tech, Computer Science & Engineering** — Kalyani Government Engineering College (2024–2026)
- 🎓 **B.Tech, Computer Science & Engineering** — Regent Education & Research Foundation (2020–2024)
- 🔬 Interested in privacy-preserving AI, Federated Learning, Medical Image Analysis and Knowledge Distillation
- 💻 Build web applications, research visualisations and embedded-system prototypes
- 🎯 Open to PhD opportunities, research discussions and academic collaborations

I am currently interested in knowledge distillation for encrypted federated training. Ongoing experiments and proposed directions are distinguished from completed research and published work in my academic portfolio.
